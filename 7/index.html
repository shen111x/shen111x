Client: Luna Anaïs Gallery

Delivery: Oct 2023


Luna Anaïs Gallery partners with Launch LA for a duo-solo show, curated by Gregory Rourke of Mystery Projects. Andrea Nakhla's futuristic humanoids and virtual reality challenge perception, while Gia Rush explores the city's zeitgeist, blending mythological elements with contemporary symbols.

"A Separate Reality" unites their visions, offering a thought-provoking exploration of dichotomies. The exhibition runs until October 15, with an artist talk on October 8 at 5 PM and a closing event on October 15 from 1-8 PM. Find us at Launch LA, 170 South La Brea Avenue, Los Angeles, CA 90036. RSVP at director@lunaanais.com.
