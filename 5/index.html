Vie Mobile Blogging
Branding & UI/UX

.


Client: Vie App

Delivery: Aug 2024


“Vie” is designed to provide a user-friendly interface and visual system that allows users to create and customize blogs effortlessly. Users can integrate multiple media formats, such as text, images, and videos, into their posts and define how their blogs behave on mobile devices. The software ensures easy navigation, enabling users to enjoy the blogging process without stress while offering a comfortable environment to store and browse their posts.

This mobile blogging software empowers users to easily create and customize blogs directly from their smartphones. It supports multiple media formats, allowing users to integrate text, images, videos, and more into their posts. Additionally, users can define how their blogs behave on mobile devices, ensuring an optimized and personalized experience for their audience. This case study explores the design process and key features that make mobile blogging both flexible and user-friendly.
