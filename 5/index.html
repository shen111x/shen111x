The LAist Radio Gala 2024 promises to be an unforgettable event, celebrating the vibrant stories and voices of Los Angeles. With a focus on community engagement, the gala brings together influential figures, artists, and local talents to honor the station’s impact on the cultural landscape.

Guests can expect inspiring speeches, captivating performances, and opportunities to support LAist’s mission. The event underscores the importance of public radio in representing diverse perspectives across Southern California.
