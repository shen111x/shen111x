Production SHENIX
Fashion Brand Identity



Client: Production Shen1x

Delivery: May 2023


Production Shen1x" is a brand dedicated to expressing existentialism and liminality through communication in design, founded on the awareness of self-existence. The company operates across various high-end sublimes, including fine art, prints, music and product design.

Its style is characterized by a youthful flair, breaking away from conventional norms and infusing products with a unique perspective and philosophy.


*concept:

The brand's theme revolves around existentialism and limitations, leading me to adopt an experimental layout and interactive approach. The software allows users to customize the brand's T-shirts. I incorporated overlapping and repetitive elements in the interface to create a stacked, breathless sensation, serving as an activating mechanism for the brand.
