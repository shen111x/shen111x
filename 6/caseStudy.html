Test Client: INAKA LA

Test Delivery: June 2024


Inaka is a dynamic sports and fitness brand, founded by Shawley Coker in 2018. It has made a significant impact in the activewear market by blending street culture with fitness. The brand is particularly known for its bold designs, featuring graphic prints on clothing such as shorts, t-shirts, and hoodies. Notable pieces include the “Inaka Ape” and “Moody Crewneck” with unique design elements.

In addition to apparel, Inaka Power offers fitness accessories such as lifting belts, wrist wraps, and knee sleeves, designed for both functionality and style. The brand also sells sports nutrition products, including pre-workout supplements formulated to enhance energy and workout performance
